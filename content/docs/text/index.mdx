Reach for this category whenever text is the shot: headlines, section titles, feature callouts, name bars. These components handle the enter/exit choreography — stagger, blur, slide, swap — so a title lands with intent instead of just appearing. For transcript-timed subtitles, use [Captions](/docs/captions) instead.

## Components

- [Text Reveal](/docs/text/text-reveal) — one staggered enter/exit animator with fade, slide, blur, scale, mask and tracking effects over characters, words, lines or blocks, plus 14 classic looks as presets.
- [Text Highlight](/docs/text/text-highlight) — animated emphasis on one span inside a static sentence: marker sweep, color shift, underline, strikethrough with replacement, or shimmer.
- [Text Swap](/docs/text/text-swap) — replaces one line with another using exit-then-enter scheduling and five transition presets.
- [Text Build](/docs/text/text-build) — words enter one at a time while already-placed words physically reflow to stay centered.
- [Word Flip](/docs/text/word-flip) — a headline types itself out, then one word cycles on a 3D flip while the rest of the sentence stays nailed down.
