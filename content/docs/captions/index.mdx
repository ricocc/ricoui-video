Use captions when someone is speaking and the words need to be on screen — talking-head clips, voiceover demos, anything watched muted on a feed. These components take a timed transcript and render frame-accurate, burned-in subtitles inside per-aspect safe areas. For decorative headlines and titles, use [Text & Titles](/docs/text) instead.

## Components

- [Word Captions](/docs/captions/word-captions) — TikTok-style word-by-word captions from a timed transcript, with pop, highlight, and color active-word styles.
- [Karaoke Captions](/docs/captions/karaoke-captions) — full-line captions with a progressive color-fill sweep across the spoken words and accent keyword emphasis.
