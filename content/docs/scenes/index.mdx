title: Scenes
description: Full scene compositions for launch and demo videos

Complete beats of a product video — openers, problem statements, pricing, and outros. Drop them into a `<Sequence>` or `TransitionSeries` and swap the copy for your own.

## Components

- [Product Hero](/docs/scenes/hero-launch) — a cinematic product-launch hero: the title and product frame push in under a soft camera move and settle.
- [Orbit Gallery](/docs/scenes/orbit-gallery) — an image-universe hero: a stream of photos flows along an Archimedean spiral from the edges into the center, shrinking and rotating along the way.
- [Pricing Card](/docs/scenes/pricing-card) — animated pricing tiers: prices count in on a tabular reel while features check off with a stagger.
- [Moodboard Reveal](/docs/scenes/moodboard-reveal) — a kinetic headline over a scattered photo moodboard that snaps together, then the camera pushes into a hero image.
