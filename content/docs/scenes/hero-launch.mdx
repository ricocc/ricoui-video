## Overview

A launch-style hero on one shared timeline: a dark product card fades and scales
into the centre, eases left until about half of it remains visible, and a white
feature card slides in from the right to meet it. Both cards then drop together
as a group while the headline reveals above — fading up from below with a blur
that resolves as the cards settle. Every beat overlaps the next, using
high-damping springs (no overshoot) for a premium, non-robotic feel. Pass your
own `heading` to change the headline.
