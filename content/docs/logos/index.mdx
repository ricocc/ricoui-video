---
title: Logos
description: Logo stings and entrances for brand moments in launch videos.
---

Reach for this category at the brand beats of a video: the opening sting, or an integrations moment mid-demo. Each component takes your mark (SVG or wordmark) and handles the entrance choreography and hold.

## Components

- [Logo Assemble](/docs/logos/logo-assemble) — a ring of image cards revolves and drains to the centre, giving birth to a simple logo that slides left as the brand name reveals to its right.
- [Logo Flicker](/docs/logos/logo-flicker) — images flip across the screen very fast, the flicker decelerates and fades, and the logo and brand name resolve underneath.
